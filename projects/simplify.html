Overview

Simplify is a calm, personal kanban board for everything you're working on. It turns to-dos into cards on a four-column board, so you can move each one from idea to done and see your progress at a glance. Reminders come to you: Simplify sends your most urgent tasks to Telegram and an AI-written summary to your inbox.

The goal is zero configuration: sign in, and your first board is already waiting. No setup, no onboarding, and about a one-minute learning curve.

Features

  • A four-column board

    Freezer, Working On, QA and Done! Every column can be renamed to match how you work.

  • Multiple canvases

    One board per project (work, home, side projects) with a folder-style switcher to create, rename and delete them.

  • Subtasks & progress

    Break a task into small steps, added right when you create it, and watch its progress bar fill up.

  • Priorities & due dates

    A colored stripe shows importance, overdue tasks turn red, and up/down arrows let you order each column by hand.

  • Telegram reminders

    Your five most urgent tasks, ranked by priority and due date, sent straight to Telegram.

  • AI email summaries

    A summary of your board written by DeepSeek and delivered to your inbox through Resend.

  • Your look, everywhere

    Light and dark themes, pastel board backgrounds, and preferences that follow you across devices.

  • Built for phones

    Swipeable columns, a floating "new task" button, bottom-sheet modals and large touch targets.

Under the Hood

  • Next.js 16 (App Router) + TypeScript: server components load the board, client components handle interaction, and the new proxy.ts convention guards private routes.
  • Supabase Auth + PostgreSQL with Row-Level Security on every table, so each user can only read and write their own tasks, subtasks, canvases and profile. A database trigger creates the profile and the first canvas on signup.
  • Optimistic updates: every change shows up instantly and is saved in the background, so the board never waits on the network.
  • Scheduled notifications: token-protected API routes, triggered by Make, build per-user DeepSeek email summaries (sent with Resend) and Telegram reminders through the Telegram Bot API.
  • Custom neumorphic design system built on CSS variables, with light/dark themes, pastel backgrounds mixed with color-mix(), and no flash of the wrong theme on load.
  • Mobile-first and bilingual: responsive down to small phones with safe-area handling, and a sign-in page in Spanish and English.
  • Deployed on Vercel with automatic deploys on every push to main.
