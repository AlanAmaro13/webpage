Overview

Unified is a personal hub that brings the tools I used to keep in separate apps into one calm dashboard: money, investments, meals, sleep, water, gym training, goals and a calendar. Every module works on its own and feeds a single Today view, so the whole picture is one glance away.

AI does the tedious parts: describe a meal in plain words and it estimates the nutrition, ask the gym coach what to do on your next set, or let it break a big goal into weekly milestones.

What you get, section by section

  • Today

    Summary cards for calories, water, sleep, budget left, goals and gym, weekly review highlights from the AI coach, quick actions, and a day navigator to log any past day.

  • Calendar

    A Monday-start month grid and an hour-by-hour day view where events span their real duration. Create, edit and delete events with a color, a description or an all-day flag.

  • Finance

    Half-month periods with income, expenses, planned savings and what's left; category doughnut, 12-month history and spending trend charts; filters, recurring templates, a savings tracker, a hide-amounts toggle and an optional PIN lock.

  • Investments

    Log each account with bank, annual rate, tax and amount, see a live net monthly profit preview and totals for money invested, monthly and annual profit. Protected by the same PIN.

  • Healthy

    Write what you ate in plain words; AI splits it into items and estimates calories, protein, carbs, fat, sugar, fiber and sodium. Review everything before saving, get AI flags and meal suggestions to close your day.

  • Sleep

    Log bedtime, wake-up and a 1–5 quality score, follow your weekly averages and a chart of every night, and edit any entry.

  • Water

    Log drinks by type and amount (water, coffee, tea, milk, soda and more) against a daily goal, with a warning when you fall behind.

  • Gym

    Ordered exercises with warm-up, working and drop sets (weight, reps, RIR), a “last time” line, a per-set rest timer, cardio with AI calorie estimates, 12-week progress charts and an AI coach whose chat is saved per day.

  • Goals

    Goals with a target date and optional metric, progress and status tracking, and AI that breaks big goals into weekly milestones shown on Today.

Under the Hood

  • Next.js 16 (App Router) + React 19 + TypeScript: server components load each module, server actions handle writes, and the proxy.ts convention guards private routes.
  • Supabase Auth + PostgreSQL with Row-Level Security; every update and delete is also scoped to the signed-in user on the server.
  • DeepSeek for meal parsing, meal suggestions, goal breakdowns, the gym coach and the weekly review. Every AI response is JSON validated with Zod and retried once on failure.
  • Chart.js wrapped in a small client component for finance, sleep and strength-progress charts.
  • Timezone-aware dates: every day-based module buckets entries by the local day, not the server's UTC clock.
  • Custom neumorphic design system in royal blue (Lora + Raleway), with a mobile pass checked in Playwright from 320px phones to desktop.
  • Vitest unit tests for dates, finance aggregation, investments, cardio and gym progress logic.
