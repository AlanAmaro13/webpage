Overview

Nova is a reading-first way to learn a language. You pick what you are studying, your MCER level (A1 to C2) and a topic, and Nova writes a brand-new passage just for you, at the right difficulty and length, with the words worth learning already highlighted.

Everything you need to understand the text sits next to it: tap a word for its meaning, ask the AI tutor about any sentence, translate on the fly, then check yourself with a quiz. Words you save come back in flash cards and in your next readings, so they stick.

What you get

  • Leveled readings

    Fresh AI-written passages on any topic, matched to your MCER level. Choose short, medium or large, or pick from daily topic suggestions.

  • Tap-a-word vocabulary

    Key words are highlighted in every reading. Tap one to see its definition, part of speech, translation and an example sentence.

  • Comprehension quizzes

    Check what you understood with 5, 10 or 15 questions per reading. See your score and retry as often as you like.

  • AI Tutor

    A draggable chat panel next to the reading. Ask about grammar, idioms or any sentence, and the tutor answers with the passage in mind.

  • Built-in translator

    Translate words both ways between your study language and Spanish without leaving the page, then save them in one click.

  • Your vocabulary list

    Every saved word lands in a searchable list that updates live. Opt in, and Nova weaves those words into your next readings.

  • Flash cards

    Review your saved words with 3D flip cards. Shuffle the deck and practice until you know each one.

  • Open questions

    Ask anything about the language, outside any reading, and get clear plain-text answers. Your history is kept for later.

  • Four languages

    Study English, Italian, German or Korean, and switch any time from your dashboard. Readings and words are kept per language.

Under the Hood

  • Next.js 16 (App Router) + React 19 + TypeScript: a shared (app) route group for the signed-in pages, API routes for every AI call, and the proxy.ts convention guarding private routes.
  • Supabase Auth + PostgreSQL with Row-Level Security on every table, idempotent migrations, and Realtime so the saved-vocabulary panel updates live.
  • DeepSeek generates readings, vocabulary, quizzes, daily topic ideas, tutor answers and translations, with language-aware prompts for each study language.
  • Draggable floating panels (Translate, Vocabulary, AI Tutor) built on a small Pointer Events hook with GPU-accelerated movement and viewport clamping.
  • AI-native design system in Tailwind CSS v4: dark-first, royal blue + violet, a twinkling starfield in dark mode, and a hydration-safe theme toggle.
  • English and Spanish sign-in page that doubles as a product tour for new users.
