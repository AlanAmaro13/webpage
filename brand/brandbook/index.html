02Idea

Inox: the tools you trust every morning.

A matte black steel thermos and an espresso at the same hour every day. Objects that are precise, quiet and used daily, never decorative. The brand behaves the same way: engineered details, one rhythm, no noise.

Machined

Every edge is decided

Flat cuts, measured gaps, one stroke system. If a detail cannot be measured, it does not ship.

Daily

Consistency over spikes

Same ratio, same palette, same voice in every post. Trust comes from repetition, like the morning shot.

Quiet

Data over adjectives

Black, steel and one warm note. Claims carry a number or a demo; nothing shouts.

03Logo

One wordmark, one mark, one favicon.

Custom lettering, no font dependency. “Amaro” is light ink; the X is heavy steel. The compact mark folds the A into the X: the A's right leg is the X's diagonal, and its own strokes stop 14 units short of the steel.

Wordmark · primary signature · from 96 px wide
Compact mark · avatars, objects, small squares · from 24 px

Construction

Shared diagonal
The X's "\" is also the A's right leg, at the X's angle (150 : 180).
Strokes 18 / 36
Light ink A, heavy steel X. The 1 : 2 ratio of the wordmark.
Gap 14
Light strokes end 14 units before any steel, cut parallel to the steel edge.
Crossbar 322
Same height as the A in “Amaro”, so mark and wordmark share a line.
Minimum size
24 px for this drawing. The favicon uses a heavier small-size drawing (light 24, gap 16) that holds from 16 px.

Misuse

Stretch or condenseScale proportionally only.

RotateThe wordmark sits on the baseline. Vertical etching on objects is the only exception.

Crema in the logoCrema is for one element beside the logo, never inside it.

Platinum X on light1.48 : 1, it disappears. Use steel #66686C.

Glow, shadow, bevelMasters are flat. Material lives in objects, not in effects.

Busy backgroundsPut a solid black panel behind the mark first.

05Type

Inter for words, JetBrains Mono for data.

Two families, both open source and already in brand/visual/fonts/. Space Grotesk is retired: the wordmark now carries the display personality. Headlines use Inter 500 at −0.045em; labels use JetBrains Mono 500 in capitals at +0.16em.

06Voice

First person, academic and close. Bilingual always.

Every technical claim carries a number or a demo. No hype, minimal emoji (none on academic pieces), co-authors and institutions always credited. Spanish and English in separate blocks.

Say

Clasificamos difractogramas XRD con un MAPE de 1%.

We classify XRD patterns with 1% MAPE.

Don't say

¡La IA más revolucionaria para paleontología! 🚀

The most revolutionary AI for paleontology!

Say

Cómo desplegué PaleoMesh en mesh.amarox.dev.

How I deployed PaleoMesh to mesh.amarox.dev.

Don't say

Especímenes mexicanos únicos (sin fuente).

Unique Mexican specimens (no source).
